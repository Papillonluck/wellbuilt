Cookie Policy

How we use cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site recognise your device and remember preferences across visits.

How WellBuilt. uses cookies

WellBuilt. is operated by Iconic CEO Limited, a company registered in England and Wales. We use cookies sparingly and only where they support a clear function on this site.

At the time of writing, this site sets one cookie:

Consent cookie — When you click Allow or Decline on the cookie banner, we set a single cookie (wb_cookie_consent) that records your choice so the banner doesn't reappear on every visit. This cookie expires after twelve months.

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, social media cookies or tracking pixels. We do not share data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not profile visitors.

If this changes

If we introduce additional cookies in future, for example basic analytics to understand how the site is used, this policy will be updated and the cookie banner will give you the choice to accept or decline them before they are set.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking cookies entirely or deleting cookies already set. Most browsers allow you to manage this in their preferences or settings. Disabling cookies entirely may affect how some websites function, though WellBuilt. itself remains usable.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to be informed about the cookies we use, to refuse non-essential cookies without consequence, and to withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies from your browser and reloading the page.
